### Packet Distribution & Authorship

Contact [fall@acf-quizbowl.com](mailto:fall@acf-quizbowl.com) to request a packet distribution. Before writing, review ACF’s [packet submission guidelines](/guidelines/packet-sub), [distribution overview](/writing/distribution/), and [category guide](/writing/categories).

If you would like to submit a half-packet or quarter-packet for ACF Fall, please fill out the [interest form](__INTEREST_FORM__) once available to request a distribution. ACF will respond with a template specifying which questions you have been assigned to write. Please keep in mind the following information:

* Before writing, review ACF’s [packet submission guidelines](/guidelines/packet-sub), [distribution overview](/writing/distribution/), and [category guide](/writing/categories).
* Please see [these questions](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1k3iR74eKPeNdoXxJRSZ-Ko-I1s-G7kBX/view) from 2023 ACF Fall for examples of target difficulty and style. All answers should generally emphasize playability over creativity; aim for creative and interesting clues with straightforward answerlines. For bonuses, aim for easy parts to have 90% conversion, medium parts to have 50% conversion, and hard parts to have 10% conversion.
* All questions should be written in 10-point Times New Roman with 1-inch margins. Tossups should not exceed 6-7 lines, and each individual bonus part should not exceed 2 lines. Please do not add extraneous spacing or paragraph breaks between question text and answerlines. For answerlines, please bold and underline minimum required answers and include the most likely answer first. Include diacritics in foreign words where applicable.
* Submissions that significantly flout the aforementioned difficulty, length, or formatting guidelines, or that include unoriginal material (e.g., plagiarized text or recycled questions from a previous set or packet submission), will be disregarded and returned to teams, with the opportunity to revise and resubmit them for a future deadline. Further penalties will be applied according to the Packet Submission Guidelines.
* Submission templates may ask for questions from specific subcategories. For example, you may be asked to write a question on a “20th-century American Poetry” instead of American Literature, or on “Economics” instead of “Social Science.” This is intended to minimize redundancy between submissions and maximize the number of usable questions from each submission. The submission form includes space to indicate your writing interests in each category, which ACF will do its best to accommodate.

Once your submission is complete, please email it to [fall@acf-quizbowl.com](mailto:fall@acf-quizbowl.com). The subject line of your email should list your team name and letter and the word “half-packet.” The Head Editor will post a list of received packet submissions, sorted by deadline, as a reply to this announcement.

Teams may outsource any part of their submission to peers from their club who will not play ACF Fall, or to alumni, as long as those individuals are not already editing, writing, or freelancing for the ACF Fall question set. *However, they must first request approval from ACF by emailing both [fall@acf-quizbowl.com](mailto:fall@acf-quizbowl.com) and [hosting@acf-quizbowl.com](mailto:hosting@acf-quizbowl.com)*. Experienced players and writers are welcome and encouraged to help their peers write questions, but ACF must know the contributors of each submission for both credit and logistical reasons.

If multiple teams from the same school are submitting packets, they should NOT be aware of each other’s questions. Any roster changes that entail an author for one team’s packet switching to a different team (e.g., if an author for Team B’s packet is moving to Team A) should be communicated via email to both [fall@acf-quizbowl.com](mailto:fall@acf-quizbowl.com) and [hosting@acf-quizbowl.com](mailto:hosting@acf-quizbowl.com) as soon as possible.

If you have any questions about packet submission, please email [fall@acf-quizbowl.com](mailto:fall@acf-quizbowl.com).

## Eligibility

ACF Fall uses the official ACF [Eligibility Rules](/rules/eligibility).

In general, teams whose players are all attending the same college/university and taking at least one class for credit during the current academic year are eligible to play ACF Fall. However, ACF also enforces further eligibility guidelines for ACF Fall to make it more accessible to new teams and players. Players are not eligible to play ACF Fall if, *at any point*, they:

* Scored more than 60 PPG in the prelims of ACF Fall, OR
* Were the top scorer (highest overall PPG) on a team that finished in the top 15% of the field, rounded up, at ACF Fall, OR
* Scored more than 60 PP20TUH in the prelims of HSNCT, OR
* Scored more than 60 PPG in the prelims of PACE NSC, OR
* Were the top scorer (highest overall PPG) on a team that finished in the top 30 at HSNCT or top 10 at PACE NSC, OR
* Finished in the top 12 in IPNCT, OR
* Scored more than 20 PPG in the prelims of ACF Nationals

Players are responsible for determining their own eligibility. If you believe that you should be eligible to play ACF Fall but are excluded by the current guidelines, or if you have any other questions about eligibility for ACF Fall, please email [eligibility@acf-quizbowl.com](mailto:eligibility@acf-quizbowl.com) to request an exemption. Otherwise, failure to abide by these eligibility guidelines may result in disqualification from ACF Fall and/or future ACF tournaments.

ACF is willing to provide the ACF Fall question set for designated high-school-only mirrors. High school teams are exempt from the above eligibility guidelines and may not play collegiate sites of ACF Fall.
