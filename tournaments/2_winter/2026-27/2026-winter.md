### Packet Distribution & Authorship

Contact [winter@acf-quizbowl.com](mailto:winter@acf-quizbowl.com) to request a packet distribution.

Please keep in mind the following information:

* Before writing, review ACF’s [packet submission guidelines](/guidelines/packet-sub), [distribution overview](/writing/distribution/), and [category guide](/writing/categories).
* Questions should be harder than those at ACF Fall but easier than those at ACF Regionals; please see [2020 ACF Winter](https://collegiate.quizbowlpackets.com/2546/) for an example of target difficulty and style. All answers should generally emphasize playability over creativity; aim for creative and interesting clues with straightforward answerlines. A majority of your tossups should conceivably be able to be tossed up at ACF Fall. For bonuses, aim for easy parts to have 90% conversion, medium parts to have 50% conversion, and hard parts to have 10% conversion.
* All questions should be written in 10-point Times New Roman with 1-inch margins. Tossups should not exceed 6-7 lines, and each individual bonus part should not exceed 2 lines. Please do not add extraneous spacing or paragraph breaks between question text and answerlines. For answerlines, please bold and underline minimum required answers and include the most likely answer first. Include diacritics in foreign words where applicable.
* Submissions that significantly flout the aforementioned difficulty, length, or formatting guidelines, or that include unoriginal material (e.g., plagiarized text or recycled questions from a previous set or packet submission), will be disregarded and returned to teams, with the opportunity to revise and resubmit them for a future deadline. Further penalties will be applied according to the Packet Submission Guidelines.
* Submission templates may ask for questions from specific subcategories. For example, you may be asked to write a question on a “20th-century American Poetry” instead of American Literature, or on “Economics” instead of “Social Science.” This is intended to minimize redundancy between submissions and maximize the number of usable questions from each submission. The submission form includes space to indicate your writing interests in each category, which ACF will do its best to accommodate.

Once your submission is complete, please email it to [winter@acf-quizbowl.com](mailto:winter@acf-quizbowl.com). The subject line of your email should list your team name and letter and the word “half-packet.” The Head Editor will post a list of received packet submissions, sorted by deadline, as a reply to this announcement.

Teams may outsource any part of their submission to peers from their club who will not play ACF Winter, or to alumni, as long as those individuals are not already editing, writing, or freelancing for the ACF Winter question set. *However, they must first request approval from ACF by emailing both [winter@acf-quizbowl.com](mailto:winter@acf-quizbowl.com) and [hosting@acf-quizbowl.com](mailto:hosting@acf-quizbowl.com)*. Experienced players and writers are welcome and encouraged to help their peers write questions, but ACF must know the contributors of each submission for both credit and logistical reasons.

If multiple teams from the same school are submitting packets, they should NOT be aware of each other’s questions. Any roster changes that entail an author for one team’s packet switching to a different team (e.g., if an author for Team B’s packet is moving to Team A) should be communicated via email to both [winter@acf-quizbowl.com](mailto:winter@acf-quizbowl.com) and [hosting@acf-quizbowl.com](mailto:hosting@acf-quizbowl.com) as soon as possible.

If you have any questions about packet submission, please email [winter@acf-quizbowl.com](mailto:winter@acf-quizbowl.com).

## Eligibility

For information on eligibility, see [ACF’s official eligibility rules](/rules/eligibility).

In general, teams whose players are all attending the same college/university and taking at least one class for credit during the current academic year are eligible to play ACF Winter. If you have any questions about eligibility, please email [eligibility@acf-quizbowl.com](mailto:eligibility@acf-quizbowl.com).

ACF is willing to provide the ACF Winter question set for designated high-school-only mirrors, provided that there is sufficient, demonstrated interest from high school teams. High school teams may not play collegiate sites of ACF Winter.
